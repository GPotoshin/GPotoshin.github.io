Bio

Comrades! My name is Georgy Potoshin. I love many things, including mathematics, physics, computer science, sailing, music, and more. I graduated from the Physics and Mathematics Department of the Kurchatov School, studied in prepa in Lyon, and earned a bachelor's degree mathematics in Lyon, was lucky enough to win a competition at ENS PSL Ulm and École Polytechnique, and am now studying at the latter. I also tried many times to complete distance learning courses at the Moscow Independent University, and actually learned most of mathematics, with the exception of analysis and linear algebra, there, but always failed either with exams or with completing coursework. However, if anyone wants to learn something from real mathematics, they can try to complete a course at NMU. It is also possible to complete courses at the Steklov Institute in a similar way.
